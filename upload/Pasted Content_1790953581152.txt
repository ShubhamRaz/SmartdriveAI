# 1. CORE CONCEPT

The application simulates three vehicle types:

1. 🏍️ Bike
2. 🚗 Car
3. 🚛 Truck

The user selects a vehicle from the website.

The selected vehicle determines which safety rules and intervention logic are enabled.

The laptop webcam is activated for driver monitoring.

The system analyzes:

* Driver presence
* Face presence
* Eye closure / drowsiness
* Driver attention
* Helmet state for bike
* Alcohol state
* Accident/impact simulation
* Vehicle state
* Safety state

The system must then make decisions and control the simulated vehicle.

The most advanced demonstration must be:

### Truck:

If the truck is moving and the driver becomes drowsy or alcohol is detected:

1. Detect unsafe condition
2. Issue warning
3. Escalate warning if condition persists
4. Disable normal/manual vehicle control
5. Activate autonomous safety mode
6. Reduce vehicle speed
7. Move the truck toward the roadside
8. Turn on hazard indicators
9. Stop the truck in a designated safe stopping zone
10. Display why the autonomous intervention happened

This should be visually obvious to a judge.

* browser-based vision is not production automotive perception
* webcam-based drowsiness detection has limitations
* helmet estimation may require a dedicated trained model for real deployment
* autonomous roadside behavior is simulated
* vehicle control is not connected to real vehicles

Do not oversell the prototype.
